Each wiki is a fully isolated database file — pages, search, and [[links]] never cross between them. Save a wiki to a file to back it up or move it; open one to bring it in. Open takes any shape Waikiki writes — a .wiki save, a wiki bundle, or a folder (or zip) of exported .md pages — and reads the file's shape, not its name.
